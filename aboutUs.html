Our Mission & Vision

At Giving New Cross, our mission is to make a positive difference in our community by offering affordable pre-loved items, encouraging sustainable shopping and supporting charitable causes. Our vision is to create a kinder, more sustainable future where everyone has the opportunity to contribute, reuse and make a difference.

What we stand for

  • Community
    • Bringing people together and supporting good causes.
  • Sustainability
    • Giving pre-loved goods another life and reducing waste.
  • Volunteers
    • Celebrating the people who give their time and care.
