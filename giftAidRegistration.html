Gift Aid Registration

What is Gift Aid?

Gift Aid is a simple way to make your donations go even further, at no extra cost to you. If you’re a UK taxpayer, we can claim an extra 25p for every £1 you donate, helping us support even more people experiencing homelessness. For example, if you donate £20, we can claim an extra £5 through Gift Aid, bringing your total donation to £25.

How does Gift Aid work?

When you donate to charity, you’ve usually already paid tax on that money. Gift Aid allows eligible charities to claim back tax from HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC), giving us extra funds to make an even bigger difference.

Your donations are already helping change lives. By signing our Gift Aid declaration below, you can help us reach more people, provide vital support, and help more people leave homelessness behind, all without giving anything extra yourself.

If you’d like to Gift Aid your donations, simply complete our online Gift Aid declaration below and help us do even more for those who need it most.
