Have a sort-out and help beat poverty

DONATE TO US

Your donations are always welcome at our charity shops in England, Scotland and Wales.

Donating items to us is simple. Drop off a bag or box of quality pre-loved items at your nearest shop, or donate to us by post.

Every donation has an extraordinary impact. Your support helps tackle climate change, respond to emergencies, and challenge inequality – fighting poverty and building a fairer world.

Thank you for choosing to donate goods to us.

Donate clothes to charity at your local shop

Our high street charity shops are a great place to donate clean clothes and accessories in good condition. We can sell clothes donations and other donated items in our shops or online to generate money to help fight poverty.

What types of clothes aren’t accepted?

We aren’t able to accept identifiable work uniforms, as these can’t be resold. We ask that you wash clothes, unless they’re brand new.

Why donate clothes to us?

Donating your unwanted clothes to charity gives them a new life. That jumper or suit sitting unworn in your wardrobe could be exactly what someone else is looking for, helping to reduce landfill and make fashion more sustainable.

Donate books to charity

Your old books can make a real difference. Every book you donate is sold to raise money that helps fight poverty worldwide, and gives a great read a second life.

What types of books are accepted?

We welcome fiction, non-fiction, children’s books, cookbooks, and textbooks in good, readable condition. We aren’t able to accept books with missing pages, water damage, mould, or heavy markings, or out-of-date reference materials such as encyclopaedias.

Why donate books to us?

Donating your unwanted books raises vital funds, keeps good books out of landfill, and helps others discover something new to read.

Donate toys and games to charity

Is there a toy that hasn’t been played with in months, or a board game missing from the family game cupboard? Pass it on. Every toy and game you donate is sold in our shops, and the money goes towards fighting poverty.

What we’re happy to take

Board games, puzzles, building sets, dolls, soft toys, cars, educational toys and video games. Please check that everything is clean, works properly and has all its pieces.

What we can’t take

Toys with broken or missing parts, toys with batteries still inside, and anything without a safety mark such as CE or UKCA. For safety reasons, we also can’t accept second-hand car seats, fireworks or reusable toy weapons.

Frequently asked questions

Can I donate without a bag?

Yes. Bringing items in a bag is easiest, and our team will help you at the shop.

Do you collect from my home?

At the moment we accept donations in our shops or by post.

Will I get a receipt?

Ask a team member in the shop and they will give you one.
