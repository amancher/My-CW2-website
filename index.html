Give Pre-loved Items a New Life

Welcome to Giving New Cross, a charity shop dedicated to supporting people experiencing homelessness. We accept donations of pre-loved items, which we sell to raise funds for our cause. By shopping with us or donating your items, you are helping us make a difference in the lives of those in need.

Empowering Our Neighbours

Every purchase and donation goes directly toward funding local support networks, and community spaces. Together, we are building a stronger, more resilient neighbourhood for everyone.

More Than Just a Charity Shop

Giving New Cross brings local people together through the simple act of giving. We welcome donated clothes, shoes,books and collectibles, giving pre-loved items another chance to be enjoyed.

Discover Something Special

From everyday essentials to unexpected treasures, our dated collections offer something different to discover. Every visit brings the chance to find a pre-loved favourite at an affordable price.

Volunteer

Join out team to learn new skills, give back and connect with your community. We offer a variety of flexible roles and any amount of time you can spare can make a difference.
